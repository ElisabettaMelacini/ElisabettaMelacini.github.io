# ElisabettaMelacini.github.io
Hello! This is my Personal portfolio site.

At five years old, I was making $10 a day reselling sweets to my classmates at a markup and explaining supply and demand to my mother. My inclinations were clear from the beginning.

What came later was a fascination with mathematics as a language for describing reality. At sixteen, during a physics laboratory project, I spent weeks trying to model a wave phenomenon that refused to behave as my equations predicted. When I finally identified the missing variable and saw the model converge with what I was observing, I discovered what still attracts me to quantitative work today: using mathematics to understand something real.

Financial markets became the natural extension of that interest. I pursued this intersection of mathematics, risk and financial decision-making in my two internships and further in my thesis where I used stochastic processes to price European options, moving from Black-Scholes toward more general models and validating the results computationally in Python. Today, I am pursuing an M.S. in Quantitative and Computational Finance at Georgia Tech, with a particular interest in financial markets and in roles where quantitative analysis meets market judgment and human interaction.

What draws me most about finance is that uncertainty cannot be eliminated but it can be measured and priced. I want to work where that analysis becomes a decision: understanding why markets move, assessing risk, forming a view and ultimately communicating that view to clients and investors.

I am currently exploring Summer 2027 opportunities across Sales & Trading, Markets and Investment-focused roles.
